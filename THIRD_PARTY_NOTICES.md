**Product:** GeDefense TV · **Version:** 0.2.0-alpha1 (VersionCode 2) · **Owner:** VisionGaiaTechnology (VGT)

Compose, no analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or networking SDK. The components below are
platform/build-time components, vendored project components, or assets whose provenance is

## 5. No third-party runtime SDKs

GeDefense TV contains no analytics, advertising, crash-reporting, attribution or telemetry SDK, no
AndroidX, and no third-party networking, JSON or serialization library. The app uses Android platform
APIs plus the two project modules (`:core`, `:tv-core`).

If you believe an attribution is missing or incorrect, please open an issue in this repository.
